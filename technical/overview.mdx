Kardy connects the loyalty programme your business runs with the memberships your
customers keep on their phones. Your team manages the programme in the merchant
portal, while customers use their wallet to join, show their QR and check progress.

Behind both experiences, Kardy keeps membership records and confirmed activity
together, so your team and your customers can refer to the same programme.

<PlatformOverview />

## The main parts

| Part              | What it does                                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Merchant portal   | Your team manages members, rewards, outlets and day-to-day activity.             |
| Customer wallet   | Customers keep memberships together, show their QR and see their progress.       |
| Shared services   | Kardy checks access, stores memberships and records confirmed stamps and claims. |
| Connected wallets | Customers can save a pass in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet where enabled.        |

## How they work together

1. A customer joins your programme and gets a membership in their wallet.
2. Your team uses the merchant portal to record a qualifying visit or confirm a reward claim.
3. Kardy checks that the action is allowed and saves the result.
4. The customer can view their updated progress in their wallet. Saved Apple Wallet
   and Google Wallet passes may take longer to update.

## One account, separate programmes

Customers can keep memberships from several businesses in one Kardy account.
Each business still has its own programme, members, rewards and team access.
Joining a programme as a customer does not give someone access to manage that business.

An **outlet** is a physical location within a business. Outlets can share the
business’s programme, while individual rewards may have their own eligible locations.

## Optional connections

Business owners can connect supported assistants to read selected programme
information through [Merchant MCP](/mcp/connect). These connections
do not issue stamps or redeem rewards.
