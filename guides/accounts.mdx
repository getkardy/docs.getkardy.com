**One email, one Kardy account.** You can use the same email and password for your
personal wallet and your business workspace. You may need to sign in separately
on each site; that does not mean you need two accounts.

<AccountHierarchy />

## One password across both sites

Your consumer wallet at **getkardy.com** and merchant portal at
**merchants.getkardy.com** use the same Kardy login—not separate passwords.

<SharedAccount />

- Resetting your password on either site changes the password for **both sites**.
  Use the new password the next time you sign in to either one.
- A password reset does not change your email, memberships, stamps, rewards,
  businesses, subscriptions or Owner, Manager and Staff permissions.
- Being a customer of a business does not give you access to manage it. Each
  business still controls its own team permissions.
- Separate sign-in sessions on the two sites do not mean separate accounts.
  Do not assume a password reset signs out every existing session. If you suspect
  unauthorised access, reset your password and contact Kardy Support.

For example, a café Owner who resets their password while using their personal
wallet must also use that new password when signing in to the merchant portal.
Their café and personal memberships stay in place.

Development and production are separate environments. A reset in a development
or test environment does not change the account on the live sites, even when the
email address is the same.

See [password recovery](/customer-journey/recover-account#one-reset-for-both-sites)
for the steps. Never share your login with employees; invite each person using
their own account.

An **account** is your personal login. A **customer membership** connects you to a
shop's loyalty programme. A **workspace** is a business you own or have been
invited to manage. Joining a shop as a customer does not grant access to its workspace.

## Where should I start?

| Your situation                              | Start here                                                       | What happens next                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| New to Kardy; want memberships and rewards  | [Create a consumer account](https://getkardy.com/signup)         | Complete your personal details, then use your wallet or join a programme. No business is created. |
| New to Kardy; want to run a programme       | [Set up your business](https://merchants.getkardy.com/signup)    | Create your login, then complete and submit business setup.                                       |
| Already a customer; want to run a programme | [Sign in on the merchant portal](https://merchants.getkardy.com) | Use your existing account. If you have no workspace, start business setup.                        |
| Already own or belong to a workspace        | [Sign in on the merchant portal](https://merchants.getkardy.com) | Open the workspace you have permission to access.                                                 |

Creating a consumer account never creates a business. Creating a login on the
merchant portal does not create the business by itself either: you still need to
complete the setup form and choose **Create organization**. It also does not
activate a paid subscription.

If you are joining an existing team, use the invitation you received rather than
creating a new organisation. Each brand has its own team permissions—being an
Owner at one brand does not give you access to another.

Looking for practical steps? Start with [Quick start](/guides/quick-start), or open
the [Staff](/guides/staff), [Manager](/guides/manager), or [Owner](/guides/owner) guide.

## Create an account

Use these steps only if you do not already have a Kardy login. Existing users
should choose **Sign in**, even if they have never used the merchant portal.

1. Enter your email and choose **Continue** on step **1 / 2**. Already registered?
   Choose **Sign in** below the button instead.
2. Enter your full name, select your phone country, enter the national phone
   number, and choose and confirm a password on step **2 / 2**.
3. Satisfy the displayed password checks, then choose **Create account**. Complete
   email verification if requested before continuing to your destination.

Phone entry accepts digits and caps the length according to the selected country's
numbering plan. The server also validates the number and selected country; a full
field is not necessarily a valid number. Supported types are mobile and fixed-line
numbers, without extensions. This format check does **not** verify phone ownership
or deliver an SMS code. Passwords are handled through the authentication service.

The merchant signup preview uses your entered name and email, but it is read-only.
Placeholder menu rows do not open settings, and the preview is not evidence that
your account has been created. The consumer signup preview shows personal details,
not a business account. Previews do not create memberships or issue wallet passes.

## “An account with this email already exists”

This refers to your **login**, not whether you have a business. Choose **Sign in
instead** and use the existing password. If you cannot remember it, use
**Forgot your password?** Do not create a second login to work around the message.

## Asked to complete your personal details?

Before using the consumer wallet or joining a programme, your saved profile needs
a name and a valid phone number. If either is missing or invalid, `/onboarding`
asks you to complete them. The email is taken from your signed-in account.

This applies to existing accounts too, including accounts first used on the
merchant portal. It does not create another login, move your memberships or set
up a business. Complete profiles skip this step. See
[complete your details](/customer-journey/account#complete-your-details-before-using-the-wallet).

## Account settings or Organisation settings?

| Where                                     | What you manage                                                                 | Who can use it                                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Account settings**                      | Your name, contact phone, avatar, and password recovery.                        | Each person manages their own account.            |
| **Organisation settings → Team & access** | Teammate invitations, Manager and Staff roles, removal, and ownership transfer. | The organisation's Owner.                         |
| **Organisation selector**                 | The brand you are currently working in.                                         | People with access to more than one organisation. |

Open **Account settings** from your account menu in the consumer or merchant
portal. Your personal details are shared across your Kardy account, not copied
into a separate login for every brand. Sign-in email is currently read-only.
See [editing personal details](/customer-journey/account) or
[recovering wallet access](/customer-journey/recover-account).

## Who can do what?

See the [role comparison](/features/team-access#a-role-belongs-to-an-organisation)
for Owner, Manager and Staff permissions. A customer membership grants no merchant
access. **Members** lists customers; **Team & access** manages employees.
