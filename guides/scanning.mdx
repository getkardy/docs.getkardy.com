## Choose the right QR

| Customer wants to…         | QR to show                        | Staff approval             |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Collect a qualifying stamp | Membership QR from their wallet   | **Slide to add stamp**     |
| Claim an earned reward     | Temporary QR from the reward page | **Slide to redeem reward** |

A membership QR cannot authorise a reward claim. Scanning either code only checks
the details; it does not award a stamp or redeem anything.

## Add a stamp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the outlet">
    Open **Scan** in the correct merchant workspace and select the active outlet
    serving the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the membership">
    Ask the customer to show their membership QR. Choose **Start camera** and
    allow access, or enter the supported code and choose **Check membership**.
    Keep the whole QR inside the camera corners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before approving">
    Check the returned member, reward and outlet. Confirm the purchase
    qualifies. Checking a balance alone is not a reason to add a stamp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slide to add stamp">
    Use the approval bar near the bottom of the screen. Drag the handle from
    left to right through all four checkpoints and release at the end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Stamp added">
    The control locks while the request is processing. Check the confirmed
    result before selecting **Scan next member**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Redeem a reward

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for a reward QR">
    The customer opens their earned reward and uses **Slide to prepare reward
    QR**. Preparing the QR does not spend stamps. See the [customer
    walkthrough](/customer-journey/rewards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and review">
    Scan the temporary reward QR at the selected outlet. Confirm the customer,
    reward and outlet eligibility. The code must still be valid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slide to redeem reward">
    Move the approval handle all the way across. Do not add a stamp just to
    access redemption, and do not use a membership QR as approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Reward redeemed">
    Hand over the reward only after Kardy confirms it. The customer's reward
    screen updates with a receipt, and the wallet refreshes its progress. The
    stamp cost is deducted once; extra stamps carry forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the approval slider works

- Drag from left to right through **four checkpoints**, then release at the end.
- Release early to return to the start without submitting.
- Checkpoint sounds are movement feedback, **not** proof of success.
- While processing, wait: do not repeat the operation or change scanner inputs.
- For keyboard access, focus the handle, press **Right arrow four times**, then
  **Enter**. **Confirm without sliding** is available if dragging is difficult.
- Sound effects are optional. Use the sound control on **Scan** or the merchant
  sound setting. Muting does not change approval or the visible result.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected; the same confirmation is still required.

## QR timer and reward expiry

A reward QR lasts **up to 10 minutes**, or until the reward's own deadline if
earlier. The customer sees the countdown. Expired codes cannot be approved.
Ask for a new code only if the underlying reward is still valid. A replacement
invalidates the previous QR and does not extend the reward deadline.

An already-redeemed result is not a second claim. Do not hand over another reward.
Read [reward expiry settings](/features/rewards#set-a-reward-claim-deadline)
for the separate one-week and one-month policies.

## When something goes wrong

- **Camera unavailable:** check permissions, lighting and screen brightness.
  Use supported manual entry if offered.
- **Code not recognised:** ask for a current Kardy code. Join-page QR codes,
  ordinary website links and malformed codes are not scan approvals.
- **Wrong member or outlet:** stop and check the correct code and outlet again.
- **QR expired or replaced:** ask the customer to prepare a fresh reward QR.
  If the reward itself expired, involve the Manager.
- **Request interrupted:** keep the current result open and check current activity
  before trying again. A network error does not prove failure. If the same
  approval can be retried, retry it rather than starting a new claim.
- **Reward unavailable at this outlet:** ask the Manager to check permitted active
  outlets. Do not bypass the restriction.
- **Access or subscription error:** ask the Owner to check access and activation.
- **Unexpected recorded operation:** retain the outlet and time for
  [Support](/help). Do not delete audit history or promise an undo.

Never share customer QR screenshots, passwords or verification codes.
