## Work the counter

Follow [Scan, stamp, and redeem](/guides/scanning) for the complete workflow.
Choose **Check membership** first, then **Slide to add stamp** for a qualifying purchase. Checking lets you inspect
a reward without adding progress. Wait for confirmation before serving the next
customer; a lost response does not prove the operation failed.

## Practise slide approval

In a training environment, review the member and outlet, drag through all four
checkpoints and release at the end. Releasing early submits nothing. Wait for
**Stamp added** or **Reward redeemed**, not just a click or animation.

For a reward, the customer first uses **Slide to prepare reward QR**. Scan that
temporary code, check the reward, then use **Slide to redeem reward**. A normal
membership QR cannot approve a claim. Expired codes need replacing; expired
rewards need a Manager's help. Never hand over a reward before confirmation.

Use **Confirm without sliding** if needed, or Right arrow four times and Enter.
Choose sound on or off on the Scan page. See the [full tutorial and rollout note](/guides/scanning).

## When to ask for help

Use the [scanning troubleshooting checks](/guides/scanning#when-something-goes-wrong).
Ask your Manager about reward eligibility and the Owner about access or activation.
Do not promise an undo or remove event history to hide a mistake.
