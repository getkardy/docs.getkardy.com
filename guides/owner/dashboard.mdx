## Start with a clear question

For example: “How many stamp visits did our first outlet record this month?”
Keep the location, dates and metric consistent before comparing numbers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the location">
    Open **Overview**. Use **Add filter** or the **Store** chip to select the
    location you want to review. Keep the all-store view when checking the whole
    programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates">
    The default is **This month · UTC**. Open **Dates**, choose a preset or edit
    the bounds, then select **Apply**. **Cancel** keeps the previous selection.
    The Overview filter row controls its totals, activity chart and location
    performance together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the activity">
    Use **Show** to switch between **Member visits** and **Rewards redeemed**.
    Inspect individual days rather than relying only on the total. Overview
    supports up to 366 days, including today's incomplete day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up on the result">
    Review **Location performance**, open the relevant member or outlet, or use
    **View live** for recent recorded events. Investigate the underlying
    activity before changing the programme.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Know what the numbers mean

| Measure                  | What it tells you                                                                      | What it does not tell you                                               |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visits                   | Recorded stamp-issued events                                                           | Unique visitors, all foot traffic or purchases without a recorded stamp |
| Rewards redeemed         | Recorded reward claims, including repeat claims                                        | Sales value, payment volume or profit                                   |
| Active stores            | The current number of active stores within the store selection                         | How many stores were active throughout a historical period              |
| Working towards a reward | Progress among loaded active members, grouped as Goal reached, On track or Not started | A forecast or a conversion rate                                         |

<Note>
  **Unavailable is not zero.** Check loading errors and retry before drawing a
  conclusion. Reporting has bounded reads; narrow a date range if the report
  cannot load completely. Demo figures are fictional, not saved transactions.
</Note>

## Compare trends fairly

The **Reward redemptions** graph in Rewards and **Successful referrals** graph in
Referrals have their own **Dates** and **Compare** controls. They use complete UTC
days and exclude today, unlike Overview. Choose 7, 30 or 90 days, or a custom
range of up to 366 complete days. Compare against the immediately preceding
equal-length period.

Hover over a day to inspect it. Click once to start a highlighted range and again
to finish; **Clear** or **Escape** removes the highlight. Highlighting does not
change the report's date filter. Choose **View data** to inspect the underlying
report. See the full [reporting guide](/features/analytics) for controls and limits.

Avoid comparing a partial day with a full day. Also consider opening hours,
holidays, closures and changes in staff recording habits before treating a rise
or fall as a change in customer behaviour.
