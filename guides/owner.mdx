The Owner looks after the programme, team access and billing. Start with the
launch guide, then use the dashboard and review guides as your programme grows.
Each page focuses on one task.

**One account can own multiple businesses, and each business can have multiple
outlets.** Start each new business with one outlet, then arrange additional outlet
activation. Businesses have separate billing, teams and member lists. See
[multiple businesses and outlets](/features/multiple-brands).

## Find your way around the workspace

Check the selected organisation before reading reports or making changes. Each
brand has its own members, programme, team and subscription.

| Page          | Use it to answer                                                       |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Overview**  | How much recorded activity is there, and which locations contributed?  |
| **Members**   | How is a particular member progressing, and who has a reward ready?    |
| **Rewards**   | Which rewards are being claimed, and who is waiting to claim?          |
| **Referrals** | Are invited friends completing their first qualifying visit?           |
| **Activity**  | What happened, at which outlet, and under which staff account?         |
| **Locations** | Are location details and the customer journey correct?                 |
| **Branding**  | Do the card, join page and counter QR represent the programme clearly? |
| **Settings**  | Are access, billing, integrations and usage allowances in order?       |
