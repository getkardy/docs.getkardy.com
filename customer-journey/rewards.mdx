<Note>
  This walkthrough covers slide approval and timed reward QRs, implemented in
  development. Check [feature availability](/availability) if your live screen
  differs.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the earned reward">
    [Open your merchant card](/customer-journey/wallet), then the reward page.
    Check the stamp goal, eligible outlets and any reward expiry date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the QR at the counter">
    Use **Slide to prepare reward QR** at the bottom of the page. Drag the
    handle left to right through four checkpoints and release at the end.
    Releasing early cancels the slide without submitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the temporary reward QR">
    Wait for **Reward QR ready**, then show staff the code. It lasts up to **10
    minutes**, or until the reward's deadline if sooner. Keep the countdown
    visible. Your usual membership QR is for collecting stamps, not claiming a
    reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let staff approve">
    Staff scan the reward QR, review the details and use **Slide to redeem
    reward**. Preparing your QR has not redeemed anything or spent stamps. Wait
    for the confirmed result before taking the reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the receipt">
    Once redemption is confirmed, the QR is replaced by a receipt and the wallet
    refreshes its progress. The reward's stamp cost is deducted; extra stamps
    carry forward between reward levels. The final claim clears the balance and
    follows the programme's reset setting. Another claim needs a new reward QR
    and approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After your final reward

The merchant chooses whether the card restarts at the first reward with zero
stamps, or becomes **complete**, with no more stamps or reward claims on that
card. This happens after staff confirm the final claim—not when you reach the
stamp goal—so you can still collect the reward you earned.
See [final reward behaviour](/features/rewards#after-the-final-reward-is-claimed).

## Prefer not to drag?

Choose **Confirm without sliding**, or focus the slider handle, press **Right
arrow four times**, then **Enter**. The same checks apply. Sound is optional:
use the sound control on the reward page. Checkpoint clicks do not mean a reward
has been redeemed; use the on-screen confirmation.

## Understand the two expiry times

- **QR countdown:** the short window for staff to scan and approve this code.
  If it expires without redemption, prepare another while the reward is still valid.
- **Reward expiry:** the merchant's deadline for claiming the earned reward.
  It may have no expiry, or expire one week or one calendar month after it is earned.
  Check the date on your reward page.

Replacing a QR invalidates the old one. It never extends the reward deadline.
If the reward itself has expired, ask the merchant for help; creating another QR
will not make it valid. Expiry does not automatically erase your stamps.

## If the result is unclear

Keep the reward page open with an internet connection. If the countdown ends while
Kardy is checking whether staff redeemed it, wait for that check; the timer alone
does not prove the claim failed. Ask staff to check their result and activity
before starting another claim. A screenshot, sound or native wallet balance is
not proof of redemption.

Deleted or changed rewards can invalidate a prepared code. Reopen the current
reward and check with staff. See [reward changes](/features/rewards#delete-a-reward).

For help, share the brand, outlet and visit time with [Support](/help), not your
private QR code. Staff can follow the [counter tutorial](/guides/scanning).
