Your memberships, one tap away. Install Kardy from your browser, without an app-store download. It opens in its own window instead of a browser tab. Installation is optional and does not create an account, business or subscription.

<Tip>
  Start at [getkardy.com](https://getkardy.com), **not this documentation site**
  or the merchant portal.
</Tip>

## iPhone or iPad

<InstallWalkthrough platform="iphone" />

1. Open [Kardy](https://getkardy.com) in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**. On some layouts, open **More** first.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**. You may need to scroll through the actions.
4. Leave **Open as Web App** on if shown, then tap **Add**.
5. Open the **Kardy** home-screen icon. Sign in with your existing account if prompted.

See [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphea86e5236/ios) for your version of Safari.

## Android

<InstallWalkthrough platform="android" />

1. Open [Kardy](https://getkardy.com) in **Chrome**.
2. Open the **three-dot menu**, then choose **Add to Home screen → Install**. The option may also be labelled **Install app**.
3. Confirm the prompt and open **Kardy** from your home screen or app list.
4. Sign in with the account that owns your memberships.

If an **Install Kardy** button is offered on the site, it can open the browser prompt instead. The browser menu works without that button. See [Google’s instructions](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en).

## Computer

<DesktopInstall />

In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon in the address bar or the browser’s app-install menu option. Confirm and open Kardy from your computer’s apps. If installation is unavailable, continue using the website.

## Check that it worked

Open the **Kardy icon**, not your old browser tab. It should open in its own window without the normal address bar. Sign in if prompted, then confirm your memberships appear and you can open your current QR.

**Done when:** Kardy opens from its home-screen icon and shows your wallet.

## Need help?

- **Inside Instagram, Gmail or another app?** Open the page in Safari or Chrome first.
- **Already installed?** Look for Kardy on your home screen or in your app list.
- **Opens as a browser bookmark on iPhone?** Add it from Safari with **Open as Web App** on if shown.
- **Asked for your name or phone number?** Follow [Complete your details](/customer-journey/account#complete-your-details-before-using-the-wallet). You do not need another account.
- **Installation unavailable?** Keep using [your wallet in the browser](https://getkardy.com/member).

Keep an internet connection for sign-in, current progress, and account updates. Installing does not enable offline stamp earning or redemption.

Adding the web app is different from [saving a native wallet pass](/customer-journey/save-wallet).

Installing Kardy does not automatically subscribe you to notifications.
