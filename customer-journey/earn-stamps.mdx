title: Collect your first stamp
description: Make a qualifying purchase and check the stamp confirmed by the store.
---

Before purchasing, ask the store what qualifies for a stamp. Joining a programme
or opening a card does not award one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the programme">
    Check that the brand appears in your wallet. If not, [complete
    joining](/customer-journey/join) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a qualifying purchase">
    Follow the merchant's published programme rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show your Kardy QR">
    [Present your code](/customer-journey/show-qr) so authorised staff can
    record the purchase at the correct outlet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Staff review your membership and use **Slide to add stamp**, then wait for
    **Stamp added**. You do not need to prepare a reward QR to earn a stamp.
    Refresh your card to see the updated progress. See the [approval tutorial
    and rollout note](/guides/scanning) if your screen differs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done when:** the staff result confirms the stamp and your current card reflects
the updated balance. Do not treat a disconnected screen as proof that nothing saved.

## Stamp not showing?

1. Ask the employee to confirm they finished adding the stamp and saw the success
   message. Scanning your QR alone does not add a stamp.
2. Open your [Kardy wallet](https://getkardy.com/member) with the account that joined
   the programme and check the correct business’s card.
3. Connect to the internet and choose **Refresh wallet**. If the button is not
   available in your version, reload the page instead.
4. If the balance is still wrong after a successful refresh, ask the store to check
   the original transaction in your membership’s activity before submitting again.

A delayed display does not mean a stamp was lost. Refreshing only checks your
progress; it does not issue another stamp. Keep the business, outlet, approximate
visit time and any error message for [Support](/help). Never send your password,
verification code or private QR.

### Why an open card may take time to update

The updated browser wallet checks after one minute, then slows to two minutes and
finally every five minutes when nothing changes. A new stamp may take up to five
minutes to appear automatically when checks succeed. Checks pause while Kardy is
hidden or your device is offline. Returning to the wallet or reconnecting triggers
a check; closely repeated checks are combined. **Refresh wallet** lets you check
without waiting for the next automatic update. If it fails, reconnect and retry.

<Note>
  The reduced-check schedule and Refresh wallet button are pending production
  rollout. Until available, reload your wallet page while online.
</Note>

This applies to Kardy in your browser or installed home-screen app, not screenshots
or saved Apple Wallet/Google Wallet passes. Native passes have separate update
timing; see [saved wallet passes](/customer-journey/save-wallet).
