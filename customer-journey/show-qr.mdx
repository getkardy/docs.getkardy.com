---
title: Show your QR at the counter
description: Present your current Kardy identity so staff can find your membership.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your wallet">
    Sign in to [Kardy](https://getkardy.com/member) with the account that joined
    the programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Show my QR">
    The universal QR works across your memberships. You do not need to select a
    merchant card first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let staff scan">
    Keep the code visible and increase screen brightness if needed. Staff use
    their selected brand and outlet to find the correct membership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm what you want to do">
    Tell staff whether you are collecting a stamp or checking a reward. Showing
    the QR alone changes nothing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done when:** staff recognise the correct membership. Staff use **Slide to add
stamp** for a qualifying visit. Your membership QR does not authorise redemption:
to claim, follow [Claim a reward](/customer-journey/rewards) and prepare a separate,
timed reward QR. See the walkthrough's availability note if your screen differs.

If the QR cannot load, choose **Refresh and try again**. Do not share screenshots
publicly. If a code may have been exposed, follow [Replace your QR](/customer-journey/replace-qr).

Next: [Collect a stamp](/customer-journey/earn-stamps) or [Claim a reward](/customer-journey/rewards).
