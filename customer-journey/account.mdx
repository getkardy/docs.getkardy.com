---
title: Update your account details
description: Change your name and phone or recover your shared Kardy login.
---

Your personal settings and login are shared across the consumer and merchant
portals—not tied to one membership or business. Use the same email and password
on both. See [accounts and access](/guides/accounts).

## Complete your details before using the wallet

If your name or phone is missing or invalid, **Complete your account** opens
before you can use the wallet or join a programme.

1. Check the signed-in email. If it is not yours, **Sign out**.
2. Enter your name and valid phone number, including country code (such as +65).
   Your sign-in email is read-only.
3. Select **Continue to your wallet** to return to your wallet or join page.

This step cannot be skipped. Correct errors or retry rather than creating another
account. Phone validation checks format, not ownership; no SMS code is sent.
Saving these details does not create a business or subscription.

## Open account settings

Select your avatar → **Account settings** in the consumer portal, or open account
settings in the merchant workspace. The consumer menu also offers avatar changes
and sign-out. For help, visit the [Support centre](https://getkardy.com/support).

## Edit personal details

Edit **Full name** or **Contact phone**, then **Save changes**. Include the country
code. **Cancel** restores saved details; avatar changes are separate.

You can clear the phone here, but the wallet requires a valid number and will
ask for it again. Marketing-email controls remain available to withdraw consent.

Your phone does not change your login. Email stays read-only until verified
email changes are supported. Merchant demo previews do not save account data.

**Done when:** a save confirmation appears and your details show the change.

## Forgotten password

Reset your password on either site to change it for **both**. Your email,
memberships and business roles stay unchanged.

Follow [account recovery](/customer-journey/recover-account) or read
[shared-password details](/guides/accounts#one-password-across-both-sites).
Never share passwords, verification codes or reset links with store staff.

For merchant-specific marketing choices, see [Email preferences](/features/email-preferences).
