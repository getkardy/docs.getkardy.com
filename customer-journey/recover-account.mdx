title: Recover access to your wallet
description: Find the correct account and reset a forgotten password without losing sight of existing memberships.
---

If memberships are missing, first check the signed-in account. Creating another
account does not bring your old memberships across.

## One reset for both sites

Your consumer wallet and merchant portal share one Kardy account. Resetting your
password at **getkardy.com** also changes the password you use at
**merchants.getkardy.com**, and vice versa. There is no separate merchant password.
Use the new password for your next sign-in on either site.

Your email, existing memberships, stamps, rewards, businesses, billing and team
roles stay unchanged. Resetting a password does not grant access to a business.
You may still need to sign in separately on each site. Do not assume that resetting
your password signs out every existing session; contact Support if you suspect
unauthorised access.

Development/test accounts are separate from production accounts, even with the
same email. Resetting one does not reset the other.

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the account">
    Use the email associated with your existing Kardy memberships.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset a forgotten password">
    Choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. Enter your own email and
    complete the verification and reset steps yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign back in">
    Open the wallet with that account and check for the expected brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for help if it is still missing">
    Contact [Kardy Support](https://getkardy.com/support) with the affected
    brand and error message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done when:** you can sign in and find your existing memberships. If you cannot
access the verification email, ask Support rather than creating duplicate accounts.

## Started signup but never entered the code?

Your account may already exist, but it still needs verification. You do not need
a different email or another account.

1. Return to **Sign in** with the email and password from your first attempt.
   Repeating signup with those same credentials also resumes the account.
2. If verification is outstanding, Kardy opens the verification screen and requests
   a fresh email. Enter the **latest** six-digit code, or use the verification link
   if that is what the screen requests.
3. If the code expired or the email did not arrive, check Spam, wait for the countdown,
   then choose **Send a new code**. A delivery failure does not delete your pending
   account; stay on the screen and retry.
4. After verification, continue to your wallet, membership join or merchant setup.
   If asked for missing personal details, complete them to finish setup.

Forgot the original password? Choose **Reset password** or **Forgot password** and
complete the email reset steps. Then sign in and finish verification if requested.
Typing a different password into signup does not replace your existing password.

Already verified? Repeating signup with the correct credentials signs you in without
overwriting your profile. Consumer and merchant access share the same Kardy account;
an existing login does not necessarily mean a business workspace exists.

If sign-in succeeds but Kardy asks you to complete your details, your account is
not lost. Finish the [personal profile step](/customer-journey/account#complete-your-details-before-using-the-wallet)
to continue to your wallet. Do not create a replacement account.

Store staff should never request your password, verification code, reset link or
email-account access. Do not include private QR screenshots in support requests.
