---
title: Track referral rewards
description: Check who joined, whether a referral qualified and which bonus stamps were awarded.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open tracking">
    Choose **Track referral rewards** in the consumer wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your side of the invite">
    Use **Friends you invited** for your referrals or **Invites you joined** for
    bonuses you received as a friend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the entry">
    Check the merchant, join date, qualification date and stamps awarded. Use
    pagination to see more results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check today's balance separately">
    Open the merchant card for your current spendable stamps. Tracking shows
    award history, not the remaining balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done when:** you can distinguish a joined friend from a qualified referral and
see any recorded bonus.

Under **Your referred friends**, entries show joining and qualification without
revealing friends' email addresses or phone numbers. **Not yet qualified** does not
promise a future award; check the [first-purchase rules](/features/referrals#what-qualifies).

If referrals are paused, historical tracking remains available. If loading fails,
retry rather than treating the error as an empty list.
