Choose the task you want to complete. Each flow explains what to do, how to check
it worked and where to go if something goes wrong. Store teams can share these
walkthroughs with customers.

New to Kardy? Start with [joining a programme](/customer-journey/join), then
[show your QR](/customer-journey/show-qr) when making a qualifying purchase.
