## Find a reward

Open [Rewards](https://getkardy.com/marketplace), branded **kardy | NETWORK**, to
browse published offers and the location map when listings are available. Choose
a category or search by merchant, reward, description, or location.
While a non-empty search is active, the map and featured card disappear: all
matching offers—including the featured offer if it matches—appear in the results.
Clear the search to restore the normal browsing layout.

## Preview the merchant

Each offer card names its merchant. Hover over the name to preview branding,
category, and listed locations. On touch screens, tap the name. Keyboard users
can focus it and press Enter or Space; Escape dismisses the preview.

Select **View merchant** to open `/shop/{merchantSlug}`. This page collects that
merchant's available rewards, terms, and locations. Sample content appears only
in explicit demo mode. Select **Join membership** there
to continue to `/join/{merchantSlug}`. A shop page is not itself a membership.

<Warning>
  Eligible published listings are available in dev; sample listings are shown
  only in explicit demo mode. The join page checks availability, and
  disconnected demos cannot create memberships. Merchant onboarding does not
  automatically publish a listing.
</Warning>
