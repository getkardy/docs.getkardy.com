---
title: Find a membership card
description: Open the right brand and check your current stamp progress.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Kardy">
    Sign in to the [consumer wallet](https://getkardy.com/member) with the
    account that joined the programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the brand">
    Open the merchant's membership card. Each brand has its own progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the current reward">
    Review the stamp balance, goal and available reward details. Refresh if the
    card was already open when staff recorded a change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done when:** the correct brand and current progress are visible. A screenshot
from an earlier visit may not reflect today's balance or reward availability.

If the card is missing, [check account access](/customer-journey/recover-account)
or [finish joining](/customer-journey/join).

## Refresh your stamp balance

After the store confirms a stamp, choose **Refresh wallet** while online, or reload
the page if the button is not available. The updated wallet reduces background
checks when nothing changes, so an open card may take up to five minutes to update
automatically. Checks pause while hidden or offline and resume when you return.
The new schedule and button are pending production rollout.

If the balance still looks wrong, follow [Stamp not showing?](/customer-journey/earn-stamps#stamp-not-showing).
Ask the store to check the original transaction before adding another stamp.

## Show one QR

To identify yourself at the counter, follow [Show your QR](/customer-journey/show-qr).
You do not need to select a brand card before showing the universal code.

## Replace a compromised QR

Use the separate [QR replacement flow](/customer-journey/replace-qr).
For a device-wallet copy, follow [Save a native wallet pass](/customer-journey/save-wallet).
