---
title: Scan, stamp, and redeem
description: Use a phone to scan the customer's Kardy QR at the correct outlet.
---

You do not need a dedicated barcode-scanner device. Use a supported phone browser,
or enter the member code using the available manual field.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the outlet">
    Open the merchant scan flow and select the outlet serving the member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the member's QR">
    The customer opens their Kardy wallet and shows their pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan or enter the code">
    Choose **Start camera** and allow camera access. Keep the whole Kardy QR
    inside the four corners with some space around it. A recognised QR starts a
    membership check; manual entry requires **Check membership**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Use **Check membership** to check an existing reward without adding a stamp.
    After checking, use **Add stamp** only to record a qualifying visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Check the result and stamp count. Do not repeatedly submit while waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redeem when ready">
    When the flow shows an available reward, confirm it with the customer and
    use the redeem action. You do not need to award another stamp to reach this
    action. Wait for the redemption result before handing over the reward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When something goes wrong

Hold both devices steady, adjust their distance until the code is sharp, and turn
up the customer's screen brightness. Tilt away from reflections. **Close camera**
stops scanning; manual member-code entry remains available if camera access fails.

Only supported Kardy membership QR formats are accepted by the camera. Website
links, join-page QR codes, scripts and malformed payloads are rejected, not opened
or executed. A correctly formatted code must still pass server-side membership,
access and outlet checks. Scanning alone never awards a stamp or redeems a reward.
Changing the code or outlet requires checking the membership again before acting.

If a redemption response is interrupted, use **Retry redemption** to check the same
request. Do not start another claim until Kardy confirms the result.

- **Camera unavailable:** check browser permissions, lighting, and browser support; use manual entry if offered.
- **Code not recognised:** ask the member to reopen their current pass and check that you selected the correct business context.
- **Access or subscription error:** ask the owner to check activation and authorised access.
- **Reward unavailable at this outlet:** ask the Manager to check the reward's eligible active outlets. Do not bypass the restriction.
- **Unexpected stamp or redemption:** retain the details and contact support. Do not delete audit history or promise an undo feature.

A QR contains an opaque identifier, not the member's email, phone number, or password.
Treat a member's QR as private and do not post screenshots publicly.
