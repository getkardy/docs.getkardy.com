Creating a merchant workspace is free. Activate a paid subscription to run your
loyalty programme.

## Growth

- **First month:** SGD 25 for your first outlet.
- **After that:** SGD 50 per outlet, per month.

The first-month discount does not repeat. Check the final price and terms at checkout,
especially when adding outlets.

## Scale

Contact Kardy to discuss a larger rollout. Scale is a tailored plan, not a published
fixed-price subscription in these guides.

## What the subscription pays for

Kardy uses Polar for merchant SaaS subscriptions. It does not process your customers'
shop purchases or pay for their redeemed rewards.

Each brand has its own subscription. Confirm the selected organisation before a
billing action; paying for one brand does not activate another.

For included allowances and monthly resets, see [Usage and capacity limits](/features/usage-limits).
