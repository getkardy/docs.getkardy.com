The network lets customers search published perks, inspect the merchant and review
the reward before joining. Merchant shop pages and rewards search use published
data; illustrations on the landing page are not actual listings.

The **kardy | NETWORK** logo identifies this discovery surface. It uses the same
Kardy account as the consumer wallet, while **kardy | MERCHANT** identifies the
business workspace. The logo does not mean a merchant has been published or approved.

Open [Rewards](https://getkardy.com/network) from the public navigation.
Search by place or perk, filter by category, and inspect available store locations.
The Singapore control indicates the current market; it is not a global market selector.
If no rewards are published, the page displays an empty-state message and account
signup action rather than substituting demo cards.

## How discovery works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the programme">
    Check the brand details, active rewards and eligible outlets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review publication">
    Use the saved listing controls and confirm the published customer view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let customers explore">
    Customers find a reward, open the shop page and choose to join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only eligible published data from active programmes is shown. An unavailable listing
is not silently replaced by a sample, and creating a workspace does not automatically
make a live public offer.

## Before promoting a perk

Check the reward wording, terms and available outlets. A signup is distinct from a
purchase or a qualifying referral visit.
