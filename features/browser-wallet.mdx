Kardy's mobile-first progressive web app holds a universal Kardy identity and
merchant-branded memberships. Each brand keeps its own reward progress.

## What members can do

- Open a brand's card and show their Kardy QR at the counter.
- Check current stamp progress and reward availability.
- Find merchant-specific referral links and referral tracking.
- Open account settings and [membership email preferences](/features/email-preferences).
- Review recent membership activity. On mobile, use **Wallet**, **Places**,
  **Rewards** and **Activity** to move between the consumer sections.

## Add Kardy to the home screen

Follow [Add Kardy to your home screen](/customer-journey/home-screen). Installation
is the customer's choice; viewing an illustration or signing up does not install it.

<Note>
  The browser wallet is the place to check current progress. Do not promise
  offline earning or automatic updates to Apple/Google Wallet passes.
</Note>

## Claims and notifications

After staff confirm a redemption, check the current membership in Kardy and refresh
if needed. A saved card image or a native wallet pass is not a live reward balance.

The public coffee-claim walkthrough uses the PWA interface and an iPhone-style
Kardy notification. It is labelled **Notification concept**: production push
delivery is not implemented. Home-screen installation does not enable claim
notifications, and the animation is not a real transaction receipt.
