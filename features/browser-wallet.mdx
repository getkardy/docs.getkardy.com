Your wallet holds one Kardy identity and merchant-branded memberships, each
with separate reward progress.

## What members can do

- Show a membership card and Kardy QR at the counter.
- Check stamps, rewards and recent activity.
- Share referral links and track bonuses.
- Manage account details and [email preferences](/features/email-preferences).

On mobile, navigate with **Wallet**, **Places**, **Rewards** and **Activity**.

## Add Kardy to the home screen

Follow [Home Screen setup](/customer-journey/home-screen). Installation is optional;
signing up does not install the app.

<Note>
  Check current progress in the browser wallet. Offline earning and automatic
  Apple/Google Wallet updates are not guaranteed.
</Note>

## Claims and notifications

### Keeping progress up to date

While unchanged, the wallet checks after **1 minute, then 2 minutes, then every
5 minutes**. Checks stop while hidden or offline and resume on return or reconnect.
Repeated requests are combined; only changed progress refreshes the page.

Use **Refresh wallet** to check immediately. This update is pending production
rollout; if the button is absent, reload while online. Native wallet passes
have a separate update process.

After a stamp or redemption, check your membership and
[recorded activity](/customer-journey/earn-stamps#stamp-not-showing) before repeating
the operation. Saved images and native passes are not live reward balances.

The coffee-claim walkthrough's iPhone-style alert is a **notification concept**,
not a receipt or a live consumer push feature. Home Screen installation does not
enable claim notifications.
