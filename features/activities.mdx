**Where:** Merchant workspace → **Activity → List view**. Available to Owners and Managers.

List view and Live are two views of the same recorded events. Use the tabs to
switch between the searchable history and the latest events beside the globe.

Action badges use green for stamps, purple for redemptions and blue for scans,
with text labels in both themes. Member names open their existing detail panel;
store names open the location page. Staff names open the matching account in
**Settings → Team & access**, subject to owner permissions. Missing or restricted
names are not replaced with guessed identities. Live also has **Show on globe**
for focusing a store without leaving the feed.
Your member, store and activity filters carry across when switching views.

## Find an event

The list starts with the ten newest recorded events. Use **Previous** and **Next**
to browse older records. Search by member name or code, then narrow by store or
activity type. **Clear filters** returns to the full list.

Each row includes the member, action, store, staff account and time. **Details**
opens the recorded event ID and staff account ID. A missing staff display name
does not erase the recorded attribution.

From **Members**, open a member's detail panel and choose **View all activities**
in their history to open List view filtered to that member. The Live feed
also links to member-filtered activity history.

## Know what is counted

- **Stamp issued** is a recorded stamp event, not foot traffic.
- **Reward redeemed** is a recorded claim, not a payment.
- **QR scanned** is a recorded scan event. Read-only membership checks and failed
  scans are not recorded in this log.

Events are append-only audit history. This page cannot edit or delete them.

<Note>
  Search needs a complete accessible member directory and currently supports up
  to 1,000 loaded memberships. If it cannot be loaded in full, clear search to
  browse events. An unavailable result is not an empty history. Demo records are
  fictional and do not represent saved customer activity.
</Note>

For the latest recorded events alongside store locations, open
[Live view](/features/live-view). For totals and trends, use
[Dashboard and reporting](/features/analytics).
