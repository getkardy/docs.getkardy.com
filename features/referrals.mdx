Owners and Managers can enable referrals and set separate bonuses: **1–100 stamps**
for the inviter and **0–100** for the friend. Members share merchant-specific invite
links and track their referral awards in the consumer portal.

## What qualifies

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite a new member">
    The friend joins this merchant through the member's referral link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the first purchase">
    Authorised staff issue the friend's first actual stamp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Award the configured bonuses">
    Both accounts must have verified emails and active memberships with active
    rewards. The programme and referrals must be active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Signup alone does not earn stamps. Self-referrals, repeat joins and bonus stamps do
not qualify another referral. The staff member cannot be the inviter or friend.
Requirements are checked at the first staff-issued stamp, not retroactively later.

The merchant's saved bonus amounts at the qualifying visit apply. A zero friend
bonus means only the inviter receives bonus stamps. Copying an invite earns nothing;
an existing membership cannot be referred retroactively. Members may reuse their
merchant invite link for different new friends.

## Set up referrals

Open **Referrals** from the sidebar. If no programme exists, choose
**Create referral programme**. Otherwise, choose **Edit referral programme**.
Enable referral rewards, set the inviter and friend bonuses, then save to return
to the overview. Optional referral settings can also be saved during organisation setup.
Pausing referrals prevents new invites and qualification; existing tracking remains.
For stamp carryover and the effect of deleting a reward, see [Stamps and rewards](/features/rewards).

## Merchant performance

The overview shows a **Live** or **Inactive** badge, the current bonus settings,
and all-time counts for friends who joined, successful referrals, and friends
awaiting qualification. Joining through an invite is not yet a successful referral:
the first qualifying purchase and staff-issued stamp must happen first.

An existing programme with no activity shows **No referrals yet**. If statistics
cannot be loaded, Kardy shows **Unavailable**, not zero. These counts do not track
link views or how often an invite was shared.

The **Successful referrals** graph is visible by default and groups referrals by their qualification date
over 7, 30 or 90 complete UTC days, or a custom range of up to 366 days, with an
optional preceding equal-length comparison. Hover to inspect a day, click a start
and end date to highlight a range, or choose **View data**. Clear or Escape removes
the highlight. Today's partial
activity is excluded. An incomplete activity read shows an unavailable message
instead of an estimated trend.

## Track rewards

**Track referral rewards** separates friends you invited from invites you joined.
It shows award history, not the current spendable stamp balance.

Follow [Track referral rewards](/customer-journey/track-referrals) to inspect an entry,
or [Join through a friend's invite](/customer-journey/join-referral) for the new-member flow.
