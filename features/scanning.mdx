Use a supported phone camera or manual code entry. A scan identifies the account;
staff still confirm the correct membership, outlet and operation.

## At the counter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an active outlet">
    Use the outlet where the purchase takes place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the customer's Kardy QR">
    Check the returned member and reward information. Do not keep a photo of the
    QR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the operation">
    Review the details, then use **Slide to add stamp** for a qualifying visit.
    For a claim, scan the customer's temporary reward QR and use **Slide to
    redeem reward**. Move through all four checkpoints and release at the end.
    Wait for the confirmed result before serving the next customer or handing
    over a reward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions and safeguards

Follow the [complete sliding and approval tutorial](/guides/scanning) for
early release, keyboard and non-drag confirmation, sound settings, retries and
expired codes. Customers follow [Claim a reward](/customer-journey/rewards) to
prepare a QR valid for up to 10 minutes. This updated flow is implemented in
development; production rollout is separate.

Authorised staff need an active brand subscription. Redemption is checked against
the reward's eligible outlets. Recorded events are audit history; retries must not
create duplicate stamps or claims. A QR contains an opaque token, not contact details.

<Note>
  Scanning in the demo does not record real events. A camera permission failure
  does not make manual code entry unavailable.
</Note>
