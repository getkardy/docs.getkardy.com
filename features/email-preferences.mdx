## Save your choice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the membership">
    Open the preferences link for the brand whose emails you want to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether to receive marketing">
    Select the option for offers and news, or **No marketing emails**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save preference">
    Wait for the saved confirmation. If an error appears, the change is not
    confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each brand has its own choice. Joining, signing in or adding a wallet pass does not
automatically opt you into marketing. Essential account emails, your membership
and earned stamps are unaffected.

## Unsubscribe from an email

An organisation-specific preference link opens a confirmation page. Submit the
confirmation to stop that organisation's marketing. This does not recall an email
already being sent. You can also change your choice in the wallet.

For merchant audiences, delivery checks and statuses, see [Broadcasts](/features/broadcasts).
