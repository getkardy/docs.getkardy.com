## Core metrics and filters

| Metric           | Meaning                                            |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Visits           | Recorded stamp events during the reporting period  |
| Rewards redeemed | Recorded reward claims during the reporting period |
| Active stores    | Current active outlets in the programme            |

Overview starts at **This month · UTC**. Use **Add filter** or the Store chip to choose a store, and open
**Dates**. The calendar has quick presets, two months side by side, and editable
start/end dates. Choose **Apply** to update totals and location rankings, or
**Cancel** to leave the current dates unchanged. One top filter row controls the
totals, chart and location performance. Ranges support up to 366 days, including
today (partial). Active stores and
member progress are current snapshots, not historical counts. Sample data is
labelled separately.

## Filter and inspect graphs

Overview's **Show** selector switches between **Member visits** and **Rewards
redeemed**, using the same dates and store as the summary cards. Visits mean
stamp-issued events, not unique visitors. The chart's vertical scale shows event
counts and its horizontal scale shows UTC dates. When today is included, the
current period is partial and comparison is against the full previous period.

Outside Overview, Reward redemptions and Successful referrals have their own
filter row with **Dates** and **Compare** chips. Choose a preset such as Last 7 days, Last 30
days or Last 90 days, or click a start and end date, then choose **Apply**.
Ranges support up to **366 complete UTC days**, ending before today; unavailable
presets and future dates cannot be selected.
These other graph dates are independent of Overview and reward/referral tables.

Graphs compare against the immediately preceding equal-length period. Remove the
**Compare** chip to hide it, or choose **Compare** under **Add filter** to restore
it. Hover to inspect a day; the tooltip follows
the plotted value. Click a start date, move across the graph, then click an end
date to finish highlighting a range. **Clear** or Escape removes the selection.
With the graph focused, use arrow keys to move and Enter or Space to set each
boundary. The selected total does not change the date filter.

Rewards, Referrals and store graphs also offer **View data** for the underlying
values. Overview offers **View live** instead.

## Activities and Live view

Choose **View live** on Member activity to open the [Live view globe](/features/live-view)
and latest recorded events. Use [Activities](/features/activities) to search the
audit history by member and filter by store or event type. These views require
Owner or Manager access; map pins represent stores, not customer positions.

## Location and member progress

**Location performance** shows visit/redemption leaders and the top three locations
by visits. Ties remain visible. Only activity attributed to an outlet belongs in
that outlet's totals. **View all locations** opens the full locations view.

**Working towards a reward** groups loaded active members into Goal reached, On
track and Not started. This is a current snapshot, not a forecast.

<Note>
  Individual location pages retain Daily · Last 30 days and Monthly · Last 12
  months views. Unlike the complete-day graphs above, these include today's or
  this month's partial activity. Location activity timestamps use the outlet's
  time zone; reporting buckets use UTC.
</Note>

Unavailable data is not zero. Complete daily history reads are bounded; if the
requested period exceeds the read limit, narrow the dates and retry rather than
treating a partial trend as complete. Do not infer revenue, conversion or profit
from visit counts alone.

For store configuration, see [Outlet management](/features/outlets). Reward-specific
claim definitions live in [reward performance](/features/rewards#understand-performance).
