## Find a member

Search by **name, member code, email or phone**. Search filters the loaded members,
not an unlimited server-wide directory; the footer shows the loaded count and
pagination displays ten records per page. If someone is missing, check the selected
brand and whether the list finished loading.

## Review activity

Open a member row or its **More details** button. The detail panel shows current
stamps, rewards ready, total visits and redemptions. Under **Activity history**, choose **All
activity**, **Visits** or **Redemptions**, then page through the results. Events
show their date and outlet; reward claims include the recorded reward name.

Use **Retry** if history fails to load. An error is not an empty history. The panel
is for inspection, not editing or deleting audit events.

Choose **View all activities** to open the [Activities page](/features/activities)
filtered to that member, with store and event-type filters.

## Rewards ready and power customers

**Rewards ready** counts one currently eligible reward per membership, not all
future rewards that excess stamps might cover. It requires an active, unexpired
membership, an active reward and enough stamps. Staff still confirm the claim
through Scan, including outlet eligibility.

**Power customers** highlights the top three among the loaded member list, ranked
by all-time recorded visits, then redemptions and name to break ties. Members with
no recorded visits are not ranked. The compact strip lives only in **Members**, not
in a separate dashboard or navigation item. Each entry shows visits, redemptions
and rewards ready. Select it to open the existing member detail panel for stamp
progress, join date, latest visit/outlet and activity history.
Searching the member table does not change the top-three ranking.
The caption gives the loaded-member scope; this is not an unlimited brand-wide ranking.

## Understand the information

Membership progress belongs to the brand, not an individual outlet. A member's
saved reward and goal determine their current progress. Historical referral awards
are not necessarily their remaining stamp balance.

Keep customer contact details inside authorised workspace tools. MCP agents cannot
read customer records, even when the owner grants all available MCP scopes.

## Capacity

See [stored-member counting rules](/features/usage-limits). Pausing or expiring a
membership does not free capacity.
