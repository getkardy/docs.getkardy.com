**Where:** Merchant workspace → organisation menu.

**One login can own multiple businesses, each with multiple outlets.**
Each business has separate billing; an outlet belongs to exactly one business.

## Separate brands from outlets

Sunday Coffee and Forma Studio are separate businesses, with their own
programmes, teams, subscriptions and outlets. Two Sunday Coffee counters belong
to one business—add them in **Locations**, not as separate brands.

## Create another organisation

Choose **Create organisation** and complete setup. The new business becomes
selected, but creating it does not charge you or activate a subscription.

Start with **one outlet**. Add others in **Locations** after setup and arrange
paid capacity before activation.

<Note>
  Single-outlet enforcement and quantity billing require rollout. If **Settings
  → Billing** has no paid-outlet control, contact Kardy.
</Note>

Each business has one subscription. Three paid Growth outlets cost S&#36;150/month;
creating locations alone does not increase capacity. Added capacity requires
confirmed payment.

Each eligible new business can receive **S&#36;25 for its first outlet's first month**,
then S&#36;50/month. Additional outlets do not restart the offer.
See [billing terms](/features/plans#first-outlet-offer-per-business).

## Switch organisations

Open the organisation selector → **Switch organisation**. It shows your role
in each business and works while a business awaits activation.

Kardy remembers your selection but checks current access on every load.
Only the selected business's Owner can manage billing.

Accepting a [team invitation](/features/team-access) adds that business to your
choices. No separate login is needed.
