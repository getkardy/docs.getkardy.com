## Separate brands from outlets

Sunday Coffee and Forma Studio are separate brands, each with its own organisation,
programme, team, subscription, and outlets. Two Sunday Coffee counters belong to
one organisation and should be added in **Locations**, not as separate brands.

## Create another organisation

Choose **Create organisation** in the organisation menu and complete setup. The
new organisation becomes your selected one. Each organisation has its own subscription;
creating it does not activate a paid plan or charge a payment method.

## Switch organisations

When you have access to multiple organisations, use the organisation selector above
the portal and select **Switch organisation**. It shows your role for each brand.
The selector is also available while a selected organisation is awaiting activation.

The selection is saved in an HTTP-only cookie, but that cookie does not grant access:
the server verifies your current relationship to the selected organisation on every
load. Billing actions also check that you own the selected organisation.

Accepting a [team invitation](/features/team-access) adds that organisation to your choices.
Use one Kardy account; separate consumer accounts are not necessary.
