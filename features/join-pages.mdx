Share a brand-wide join link or print a store-specific sign. Customers sign in or
create an account, complete verification and join the available programme.

## Before sharing

Review the saved brand design, reward wording and available outlets. Use the join-page
preview to inspect the customer experience. Previewing does not enrol a customer or
activate an unpaid programme. To produce printed artwork, use [QR signs](/features/qr-signs).

## Edit the shared brand profile

The join page inherits its logo, primary colour and content from **Branding →
Brand profile**. There is no separate join-page colour override. The business name
comes from **Settings → Business details** and is read-only here.
Use the Settings link to rename it. In Brand profile, choose **Upload logo** or
**Replace logo**, select a PNG, JPG or WebP up to 2 MB, then choose **Save brand profile**.
The saved logo is a public brand asset. Demo uploads only change the preview.

The membership preview uses your saved wallet-pass design. It does not issue an
Apple Wallet or Google Wallet pass; native passes require their separate setup.

## Joining behaviour

A store-specific link records the new membership's signup outlet. Reopening it does
not overwrite that first attribution. A referral link can carry the inviter too,
but joining is not proof of a purchase and does not trigger referral bonuses.

<Note>
  The sign QR opens a join page. It is different from the private QR that a
  customer presents for scanning. Programme availability and member capacity are
  checked when joining.
</Note>
