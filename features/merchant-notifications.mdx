## Included usage

**Growth: 1,000 PWA push notifications per paid outlet per UTC month. Scale: contact us.**
Pooled across the business: three paid outlets share 3,000 notifications.
Only payment-confirmed outlets add capacity; creating a location does not.
Changing outlet quantity does not reset usage. If a reduction puts usage over the
new limit, new alerts pause until capacity is available again.

One event queued for two devices uses two notifications, even if delivery fails.
Retries do not count again. Unused capacity does not carry forward.

At the cap, new alerts pause until **00:00 UTC on the first of the next month**.
Loyalty operations continue, with no overage charges or replay of missed alerts.

Owners can open **Settings → Usage & limits → Notifications**:

| Counter        | Meaning                                                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sent           | Accepted by the push service this month—not confirmed displayed or read. |
| Allowance used | Device copies queued this month, including failed deliveries.            |
| Remaining      | Unused monthly allowance.                                                |
| Monthly limit  | The business's configured allowance.                                     |

Select **Refresh usage** for current totals. Sent counts by send month; allowance
counts by queue month. Adding outlets, removing devices or clearing logs does not
reset or multiply capacity. Sent history starts when tracking is enabled.

## Enable on your device

1. Select your business and open **Settings → This device**.
2. Choose alert types, then **Enable notifications**.
3. Allow the browser prompt. Use **Save notification settings** for later changes.

On iPhone/iPad, install Kardy on your Home Screen and open it there; iOS/iPadOS
16.4+ is required. Supported Android and desktop browsers need permission and HTTPS.

## Businesses, devices and privacy

Settings apply to **one business on one device**. Repeat setup elsewhere.
Each person can register five devices per business; save again within 90 days
to keep a registration active.

**Turn off for this business** affects only that business/device. Merchant-site
sign-out disables all your merchant push registrations; sign in and enable again.
Consumer-site sign-out does not affect them.

Removed access or inactive billing stops new deliveries. Already-sent alerts
cannot be recalled. Alerts contain no customer names or contact details;
opening one checks access to the business.

## Why didn't I receive an alert?

Delivery may take a minute or longer, even with Kardy closed. Check:

- The business, device and alert type are enabled, with allowance remaining.
- Browser permissions and Focus/Do Not Disturb allow notifications.
- On iPhone/iPad, you are using the installed Home Screen app.
- After sign-out, a device change or expiry, notifications are enabled again.
- If permission was blocked, allow it in device/browser settings and reload.

Check [Members](/features/members) or [Activity](/features/activities) before
repeating an operation. A missing alert does not mean a stamp or redemption failed.

These alerts are separate from [broadcasts](/features/broadcasts), marketing
emails and [customer wallet updates](/features/browser-wallet).
