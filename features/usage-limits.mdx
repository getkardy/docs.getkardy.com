## Read your usage overview

Open **Settings → Usage & limits** as the organisation Owner. The overview lists
**MCP tool calls**, **Broadcast recipients**, stored members, active outlets,
team members and stored rewards. Each row shows **used / limit**, a capacity bar,
and a warning when nearing or reaching the limit.

Below the table, **MCP usage** and **Broadcast usage** each show **Used**,
**Remaining**, **Monthly limit**, and the next reset date. Remaining capacity never
displays below zero. **No allowance** means the workspace has no configured capacity
for that resource; it does not mean unlimited usage.

Use **Refresh usage** to request the latest totals. Monthly usage covers the current
UTC calendar month, not a rolling 30-day window. Daily usage history and historical
date filters are not available yet. These usage totals are separate from visit,
reward-redemption and referral performance charts.

The demo shows clearly labelled illustrative September 2026 usage. Its sample
allowances are not included-plan promises. Use your live workspace for actual limits.

## Growth defaults

| Resource           | Allowance per workspace                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Stored members     | 1,000, including paused and expired memberships     |
| MCP tool calls     | 1,000 per UTC calendar month                        |
| Active outlets     | 1                                                   |
| Staff and managers | 10, excluding the Owner unless also listed as staff |
| Stored rewards     | 25, including archived history                      |
| Email recipients   | 0 until separately agreed and configured            |

Scale uses agreed finite allowances, not unlimited capacity. Adding an outlet or
another agent does not automatically multiply allowances.

## At the limit

The usage page warns at 80%. New capacity-consuming operations stop at the cap;
existing memberships and earned rewards are not automatically deleted. There are
no automatic overage charges. Contact Kardy to arrange additional capacity.

Monthly MCP and email counters reset on the first day of the month at **00:00 UTC**,
not on the subscription renewal date. Stored-member capacity does not reset monthly.
Switching keys, reconnecting OAuth or changing member status does not reset usage.

## What counts

- **MCP:** each authorised tool request and results page counts once. Manual keys
  and OAuth clients share the counter. Connection setup and discovery do not consume
  monthly calls; separate burst limits still apply.
- **Email:** each recipient reserves allowance when queued, including recipients
  later suppressed or failed. Retries do not reserve it again. An over-budget batch
  is rejected rather than partially queued.
- **Stored capacity:** paused or expired memberships and archived rewards still count.
  Existing over-limit records remain, but further capacity-consuming additions stop.

An active subscription is still required for paid operations. An allowance alone
does not enable email delivery or activate an unpaid programme.
