## Basic merchant push

Alerts cover **new members and reward redemptions**, not stamps or customer
marketing. The allowance is shared across outlets and recipients: one event
allocated to two devices uses two notifications. Failed sends are not automatically retried.

Owners can check **Usage & limits → Notifications** for Sent, Allowance used,
Remaining and Monthly limit. **Sent** means accepted by the push service, not
displayed or read. Sent counts by send month; allowance counts by allocation month.
See [notification setup and limits](/features/merchant-notifications).

## Employee accounts explained

**Managers and Staff share one allowance; Owners and customer Members are excluded.**
Two Managers plus three Staff use five accounts.

Where pooled billing is enabled, Growth allows **5 × paid outlets**. Employees
can work across outlets and count once per business; no outlet assignment is
required. Creating a location or requesting more capacity does not add slots.

After a capacity reduction, existing employees remain, but new employees cannot
join until the team fits the limit. Inactive subscriptions block paid operations.
See [roles and permissions](/features/team-access).

## Business and outlet explained

One account can own several businesses. Each business has its own subscription,
programme and member list; each outlet belongs to exactly one business.
“Workspace” and “organisation” also mean business.

Growth bills **S&#36;50 × paid outlets/month**: three outlets cost S&#36;150.
Use **Settings → Billing** where enabled, or contact Kardy. Added capacity requires
payment confirmation; member, MCP and reward allowances do not automatically multiply.
See [billing](/features/plans).

## Check your usage

The Owner can open **Settings → Usage & limits** and select **Refresh usage**
for current totals. MCP usage includes remaining calls and the next reset date;
notifications show the separate counters above.

Monthly counters follow the **UTC calendar**, not your billing cycle.
Daily history and date filters are unavailable. **No allowance** means zero
capacity; demo figures are examples, not entitlements.

## At the limit

Warnings appear at 80%. New capacity-consuming operations stop at the cap, but
existing memberships and earned rewards remain. There are **no automatic overage
charges**. Contact Kardy for more capacity.

Monthly counters reset on the first at **00:00 UTC**. New keys, OAuth
reconnections and membership-status changes do not reset usage.

## What counts

| Resource          | What counts or frees capacity                                           | At the limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| MCP calls         | Authorised tool requests and results pages; monthly reset.              | Counted calls stop until reset or an agreed increase.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Notifications     | Each allocated device copy, including failed deliveries; monthly reset. | Growth: 1,000 × payment-confirmed outlets, pooled per business. New alerts pause at the cap; loyalty operations continue. No rollover or replay. Creating locations or changing devices does not add capacity or reset usage. |
| Employee accounts | Managers and Staff; removal frees a slot, changing roles does not.      | Additional invitations cannot be accepted; existing accounts remain.                                                                                                                                                          |
| Stored rewards    | Saved definitions, including archived rewards; no monthly reset.        | Additional definitions cannot be saved. Review deletion effects before removing rewards.                                                                                                                                      |
| Stored members    | Active, paused and expired memberships; no monthly reset.               | New joins stop at the configured cap; existing memberships and rewards remain.                                                                                                                                                |
| Active outlets    | Enabled locations; no monthly reset.                                    | Extra locations require confirmed paid capacity.                                                                                                                                                                              |

### Examples

- **Employees:** two Managers and three Staff use five accounts; the Owner is separate.
- **MCP:** agents using 600 and 400 calls share the 1,000-call allowance. Setup
  and discovery do not count; separate request-rate limits still apply.
- **Rewards:** one “Free coffee” definition counts once, regardless of redemptions.
