Choose your logo, brand colour and saved card design. Browser membership cards and
join pages use that design; branding does not change the identity behind the QR.

## What you can change

- Business name in **Settings → Business details**; logo and primary brand colour
  in **Branding → Brand profile**. These are shared branding, not separate pass identities.
- Saved background family, gradients/patterns, secondary colour and chop shapes.
- Stamp-number display within the supported card design.

## Shared profile and overrides

**Brand profile** owns the shared logo, primary colour, category, tagline and
description. The join page inherits these fields; it has no independent colour
or logo editor. Profile edits preview across Branding tabs; choose **Save brand
profile** to publish them. Demo edits are not saved.

**Pass designer → Shared pass appearance** controls the secondary colour, gradients,
patterns and stamp styling. These affect cards, including cards embedded on the
join page—not the join-page background or sign accents. Printed **QR signs** use
Kardy's fixed colours and branding. You can customise their text, not their
colours; these text changes do not change the shared brand profile.

Reward-specific icon choices and preview stamp counts are illustrative. They do
not issue stamps or create redemption events. Native wallet passes have different
styling and update boundaries.

## Use this feature

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct brand">
    Select your organisation, then open Branding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the wallet-pass design">
    Change supported design controls and switch between **Apple Wallet** and
    **Kardy mobile app** previews. The mobile app is Kardy's browser/PWA card,
    not a separate app-store download. Use **Preview stamps** to inspect
    progress. Both modes share one design and preview stamp count; switching
    modes does not reset either. The Kardy app is the default preview. Choose
    **Save pass design** once to save the shared design. Join-page previews use
    the same draft artwork.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check the customer view">
    Open the browser membership or join page to verify the saved result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other Branding tabs

- [QR signs](/features/qr-signs): choose a format, outlet and print layout.
- [Customer join pages](/features/join-pages): inspect the joining experience.
- [Native wallet passes](/features/native-wallets): separate signing and update requirements.

Use the switches for **Show stamp numbers** and **Publish in Kardy discovery**
where available, then save your changes. Discovery is labelled **App only** because
it controls public Kardy listings, not native-wallet installation. Demo previews do not save. The Wallet
pass preview is not proof that a native pass has been issued or installed;
its sample QR cannot identify a real membership.
