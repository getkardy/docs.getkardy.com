## Implemented in the development environment

The configured dev backend supports consumer sign-in and joining, browser memberships
and QR identity, merchant setup, stamps/redemption, referral rewards, saved branding,
member and outlet reporting, published listings, team access and multiple brands.
Production rollout and authenticated end-to-end verification are separate.

## Additional gates and known boundaries

### Final reward: Reset / No reset

The merchant choice for what happens [after the final reward is claimed](/features/rewards#after-the-final-reward-is-claimed)
is implemented. **Reset** restarts at the first active reward with zero stamps;
**No reset** completes the card and blocks further stamps and stamp-reward claims
after the final redemption. Reaching the goal alone does not complete the card.
Completed cards remain complete after later configuration changes.
Owners and Managers can also pause reward collection when stock runs out without
stopping stamp earning or extending existing reward deadlines.

### Slide approval and timed reward QRs

The [counter approval tutorial](/guides/scanning) and
[customer claim tutorial](/customer-journey/rewards) describe the development
implementation: four-checkpoint sliding, a non-drag alternative, optional sounds,
single-use reward QRs valid for up to 10 minutes, and earned-reward deadlines.
Production requires a coordinated backend and consumer/merchant rollout. These
guides do not establish that the updated flow is already live.

The account menu, animated dashboard, stamp cards and phone screens on public
pages are demonstrations. Entering details in a signup preview is not a saved
profile, opening a sample card is not joining, and a notification illustration
does not mean a push message was sent.

## Branding and hosting

The product is **Kardy**, using **getkardy.com** as its chosen public domain.
The merchant workspace uses `merchants.getkardy.com`; documentation is intended for
`docs.getkardy.com`. These configured destinations do not establish that DNS,
hosting, auth redirects or email delivery have been activated.

Kardy's logo pairs the outlined wordmark with a slightly enlarged card mascot on
its right. Merchant, Network and Documentation labels retain their divider and
small, spaced lettering. The main site background stays white, with pink and
violet accents. Merchant-owned logos and card colours stay separate from Kardy's
branding.
