Reviewed against the repository on **13 September 2026**. Your deployed version may
differ. This page records rollout gates; [Features](/features/overview) explains
controls and behaviour. A landing-page animation is not proof of activation.

## Implemented in the development environment

The configured dev backend supports consumer sign-in and joining, browser memberships
and QR identity, merchant setup, stamps/redemption, referral rewards, saved branding,
member and outlet reporting, published listings, team access and multiple brands.
Production rollout and authenticated end-to-end verification are separate.

## Additional gates and known boundaries

The account menu, animated dashboard, stamp cards and phone screens on public
pages are demonstrations. Entering details in a signup preview is not a saved
profile, opening a sample card is not joining, and a notification illustration
does not mean a push message was sent.

| Capability                                 | Check before promising availability                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [Programme operations](/merchants/billing) | Require authorised access and an active subscription. Free workspace creation does not activate operations.                                                                                                              |
| [Capacity](/features/usage-limits)         | Enforced in dev. Additional outlets and email require explicit allowances.                                                                                                                                               |
| [Kardy network](/features/kardy-network)   | Only eligible, published live data appears. Setup alone does not publish a listing; samples require explicit demo mode.                                                                                                  |
| [Email broadcasts](/features/broadcasts)   | Drafts, consent and the queue are implemented. Sending starts disabled; paid provider setup, server/backend gates and recipient allowance are required. No live scheduler or delivery-confirmation webhooks are enabled. |
| [Merchant MCP](/features/merchant-mcp)     | Read-only OAuth and manual-key access is implemented in dev. Owner approval and an active subscription are required; there is no consumer MCP.                                                                           |
| [Team access](/features/team-access)       | Share invitation links manually; automatic invitation emails are not connected. Ownership verification needs working auth email delivery.                                                                                |
| [Native wallets](/features/native-wallets) | Signing is implemented but issuance starts disabled until issuer credentials and scanner readiness are verified. Automatic pass updates are not implemented.                                                             |
| PWA claim notifications                    | The iPhone-style coffee-claim notification is a labelled concept preview. Production push delivery is not implemented. Confirm redemption through staff and current Kardy data.                                          |
| [Card design](/features/branded-cards)     | Saved browser designs are supported in dev. Reward-specific icons and preview stamp counts remain illustrative.                                                                                                          |
| [Reporting](/features/analytics)           | Date-filtered totals and complete-day graphs are connected in app code. Incomplete reads show unavailable; authenticated deployment verification is separate. Demo charts use sample data.                               |

[Activities and Live view](/features/live-view) are implemented in app code:
searchable audit history and a store globe with checks every 15 seconds while
visible. This is not an instant stream or customer-location tracker. Demo feeds
are static; authenticated deployment verification is separate.

For an account-specific problem, use [Help](/help). Do not share an Owner's login or
assume a successful preview is a saved transaction.

## Branding and hosting

The product is **Kardy**, using **getkardy.com** as its chosen public domain.
The merchant workspace uses `merchant.getkardy.com`; documentation is intended for
`docs.getkardy.com`. These configured destinations do not establish that DNS,
hosting, auth redirects or email delivery have been activated.

The production backend's display name is now Kardy. The persistent development
branch remains separate. Renaming the product did not rotate QR identifiers,
credentials or issued passes.
