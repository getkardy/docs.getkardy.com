## What is Kardy?

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/wallet-overview.svg"
    alt="Illustrative branded cards in one Kardy browser wallet, with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet support when native pass issuance is enabled."
  />
</Frame>

Kardy helps your business run a branded loyalty programme across its outlets.
Businesses create branded membership cards, award stamps, and offer rewards.
Members keep their cards in Kardy and show their QR when they visit—no app-store download needed.

**Your brand. One wallet. No extra app.** Customers keep memberships together;
you manage your own rewards and outlets. This describes the Kardy browser wallet,
not automatic Apple Wallet or Google Wallet installation. Kardy also supports
[Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes](/features/native-wallets) when native
issuance is configured and enabled. Members choose to save their pass; live stamp
progress stays in Kardy.
